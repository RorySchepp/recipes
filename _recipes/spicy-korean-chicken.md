## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 Tbsp cooking oil, for pan-frying

**Marinade**

- 2 Tbsp oil
- 1 1/2 Tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 1 tsp gochugaru (Korean chili powder), or regular chili powder
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 1/2 Tbsp sugar
- 1 inch ginger, peeled and minced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- Salt, to taste

## Method

1. Cut the chicken thighs into smaller pieces. If they're thick,
   slice each one in half horizontally.
2. In a bowl, mix together all the marinade ingredients. Add the
   chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours.
3. Heat the cooking oil in a skillet. Once hot, pan-fry the chicken
   until cooked through and slightly charred.
4. Serve right away with steamed rice.

## Notes

- To bake instead, cook at 400°F for about 20 minutes. It can also
  be grilled or air-fried.
- Thighs stay juicier than breasts. Breasts work but cook faster
  and can dry out.
- Use sugar, not honey, which burns more easily.
- For a milder version, use less gochujang and skip the chili powder.
- Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge, or freeze after cooking.
