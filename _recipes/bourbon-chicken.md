## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken thighs
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- Black pepper
- 1 Tbsp avocado oil
- 1/3 cup soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 Tbsp ketchup
- 1/2 cup apple juice
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1/3 cup light brown sugar
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 Tbsp cornstarch, dissolved in water

## Method

1. In a bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, hoisin, ketchup, apple
   juice, water, vinegar, brown sugar, ginger and garlic. Set aside.
2. Season the chicken thighs with garlic powder, onion powder,
   paprika and black pepper. Make sure to cover every part.
3. Add the chicken to a skillet with the avocado oil, letting
   both sides brown. It doesn't need to be cooked through at this
   point. Remove and let rest.
4. Add the sauce to the pan and scrape up any fond. Let it reduce by
   half before adding the cornstarch slurry.
5. Add the chicken back in and let it all simmer until it comes up
   to temp.
